# P0 — Healthcare Administration Essentials

## Scope

Intact remains a general-purpose DOCX editor. This increment adds an administrative starter set for healthcare organisations; it does not turn Intact into an electronic health record, clinical decision-support system, records-management system or compliance-certified product.

## Included templates

The start page has a separate **Healthcare administration** group:

- Healthcare policy
- Standard operating procedure
- Patient administration form
- Discharge administration checklist
- Incident report
- Patient complaint record
- Healthcare quality audit
- Corrective action record (CAPA)

Each document is generated in the browser as a normal editable DOCX. The templates provide document-control fields (organisation, department, document ID, version, owner, effective date and review date), structured sections and tables where useful, and a common pre-approval / pre-sharing checklist.

## Privacy and safe handling

The templates remind staff to use the minimum necessary information, verify recipients and authorisation, and avoid putting clinical assessments into administrative forms. Before releasing a DOCX, users should also use Intact's existing **Review → Inspect document** / **Before you release** panel. It inspects document-level information such as comments, tracked changes, hidden text and personal document properties, and offers supported removal actions.

This is a review aid, not automated patient-data detection, guaranteed de-identification, legal advice or a compliance certification. It cannot establish whether a user is authorised to disclose a record, whether a recipient is appropriate, or whether the organisation's deployment and operating procedures meet HIPAA, Japanese APPI, or other applicable requirements. Staff must follow local policy and use approved systems for patient records.

## Form and workflow boundaries

The patient administration and discharge checklists are administrative aids. They intentionally do not collect diagnoses, prescribe care, determine clinical readiness for discharge, or validate clinical decisions. Clinical information and decisions belong in the organisation's approved clinical record and workflow.

Approval names and dates in these starter documents are placeholders, not an electronic-signature or approval workflow. Organisations should configure their own controlled-document approval process.

## Acceptance checks

- The start page presents all eight templates together in their own group.
- Each template opens as an editable DOCX with its title and expected sections.
- Each includes document-control placeholders and the pre-approval / pre-sharing checklist.
- Existing document inspection remains available to check comments, revisions, hidden text and document properties before download.
- The generated documents remain ordinary Word-compatible files and can be edited and downloaded using the existing editor workflow.
